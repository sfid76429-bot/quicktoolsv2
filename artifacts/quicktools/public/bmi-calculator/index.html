BMI is a population-level screening measure and does not account for every body type or health factor.

How BMI is calculated

For metric measurements, BMI is weight in kilograms divided by height in meters squared. For imperial measurements, the calculator uses weight in pounds, height in inches and the standard 703 conversion factor.

The category labels are broad adult reference ranges. For personal health decisions, talk with a qualified clinician rather than relying on a single number.
