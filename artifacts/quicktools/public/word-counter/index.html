How to use this word counter

Paste an essay, product description, social caption or any other text into the box. QuickTools counts words separated by whitespace, characters both with and without spaces, complete sentences and a rough reading time instantly as you type.

Because the counter runs locally in your browser, your draft is not uploaded for analysis. This makes it a practical free word counter for private notes and client copy as well as everyday writing.
