Why this exists

Helpful, without the heavy machinery.

QuickTools is a small collection of browser-first utilities for the jobs that do not deserve a new app, an account or a data upload.

Privacy is a feature, not a footnote.

QuickTools processes text, calculations and image files in your browser wherever the tool allows it. Nothing is sent to a QuickTools server, and there are no accounts, cookies or telemetry scripts in this static site.

How to use it

Choose a tool, enter your information, and use the result. When a tool offers a download, the file is created locally on your device. For sensitive information, you should still use your own good judgement and keep your browser up to date.

A quiet set of tools

There are no inflated promises here. The goal is dependable little helpers: clear controls, useful error messages, keyboard-friendly forms and layouts that work just as well on a phone.

QuickTools. Made for the small stuff.
